Starting this blog

Posted August 22, 2026

I set up this space to keep notes on what I am reading and building  —  mostly federated learning and unlearning, with the occasional detour into edge systems.

Why write things down

Papers report the result that worked. The reasoning that got there, and the attempts that did not, usually go unrecorded. This is where I keep that part.

What to expect

Short posts: a paper I found useful, a derivation I had to work through twice, or a bug that cost me a day.
